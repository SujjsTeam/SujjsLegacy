## 1.0.0

First release of Sujjs Legacy. [Full notes](changelogs/1.0.0.md)

- Absolute Reactor endgame multiblock with laser animation
- Modular Machinery recipes reworked through machines from other mods
- Gem Drill and Chaos Drill for Silent Gems and Silent Gear ores
- Duplicate ores removed, Sujjs's Ores as the main ore source
- Homes for singleplayer and multiplayer
- Quests, custom main menu and crash reporting to GitHub
