# Known Mod Issues

Problems that come from the mods themselves and cannot be fixed by the modpack. If your problem is listed here, there is no need to open an issue.

| Mod | Issue | Workaround |
|---|---|---|
| Silent Gear | Some grip and binding blueprint or template recipes do not load | Use the other grip and binding types |
| Design n' Decor | Some Text Plate variants have no model and can look invisible from some angles | None. Wait for a mod update |
| CMDCam | Camera keys come unbound to avoid conflicts | Set them in Options > Controls if you use the mod |
| Modular Machinery Reborn | The Absolute Reactor uses End Crystals for its laser effect | Do not build the reactor in the End, where the crystals can interfere with the dragon fight |

## Logs that look scary but are harmless

These lines show up in `latest.log` and do not affect the game:

- Loot tables and recipes for mods that are not in the pack
- Shader warnings from Draconic Evolution on Veil
- Model warnings from Scorched Guns on GeckoLib
- Physics tag warnings from Sable
