# Sujjs Legacy 1.0.0

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.265.

First release.

## Endgame

- Added the **Absolute Reactor**, a Modular Machinery Reborn multiblock that forges **The Absolute**.
- While the reactor is crafting, the four towers fire lasers into the central tower. The effects grow stronger as the craft progresses and end with a light burst when The Absolute is done.
- The reactor controller is made through Draconic Evolution Chaotic Fusion Crafting.

## Modular Machinery Reborn

- Every hatch and casing recipe now uses machines from other mods: Create Mechanical Crafting, Industrial Foregoing Dissolution Chamber, Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus, Mystical Agriculture Infusion, Modern Industrialization Assembler and Draconic Evolution Fusion Crafting.
- Each tier needs the previous hatch and an upgraded part:
  - Item hatches: Sophisticated Storage chests
  - Fluid hatches: Mekanism Fluid Tanks
  - Energy hatches: Mekanism Energy Cubes
  - Source hatches: Ars Nouveau Source Jars
- The Blueprint is now made through Draconic Fusion Crafting.

## Ores and world generation

- Sujjs's Ores is the main source of common ores. Duplicate ores from other mods no longer generate.
- Silent Gems and Silent Gear ores no longer generate in the world.
- Added the **Gem Drill** and the **Chaos Drill** for the Modern Industrialization Quarry. They mine Silent Gems gems, Chaos Ore, Crimson Iron, Bort and Azure Silver.
- Oritech oil springs no longer generate.

## Recipes

- The Oritech Arcane Catalyst is now made in the Ars Nouveau Enchanting Apparatus with endgame materials.
- Added Scorched Guns compatibility recipes for Mekanism, Immersive Engineering, Create Crafts & Additions and Create Mechanical Crafting.
- Fixed Mystical Agriculture essence recipes for tin, silver, lead, nickel, platinum, uranium, iridium, zinc, aluminum, steel, bronze, brass, electrum, constantan, invar, ruby, sapphire, sulfur and silicon.

## Quality of life

- Added `/sethome`, `/home` and `/delhome`. Works in singleplayer and multiplayer without cheats, and every player has their own homes.
- Starting guide books are no longer given when joining a world. They can still be crafted.
- Reorganized default keybinds to avoid conflicts. Armor and ability modes use Alt combinations.
- Custom main menu.
- Crash Assistant now helps players report crashes on GitHub.
